Money 2.4 — Save more 🐷
• Grow → Your money: your 50/30/20 split this month, Money health score, what's left at month end, how long your money lasts if income stopped, and the round-up jar
• 50/30/20: Needs · Wants · Saved against the rule you pick (50/30/20, 60/20/20, 70/20/10 or Pay yourself first). Tap any category to move it between needs and wants
• Money health: one score out of 100 from saving, emergency fund, EMIs, budget and goals — tap a part to see why, with one tip each
• Leak finder: subscriptions (mark the ones to cancel), bank charges, food delivery, small daily spends — what each costs in a year and in 10 years
• Wait-list: park something you want for 7 or 30 days. Money asks again when time's up — skip it and it counts as saved
• True price of a habit: pick a repeat spend, slide “cut by”, see a year's saving and what it becomes in 10 years (with a graph)
• Hours of work: see any price as hours of your work — optionally on every payment and the wait-list
• Future you: what monthly investing becomes at 30, 40, 50, and when you reach your freedom number
• Salary-day split: the moment your salary SMS arrives, one notification → save first, set the budget, fill your goals
• Round-up jar: every payment rounded up to ₹10 / ₹50 / ₹100, the spare change saved for a goal
• Big payments now say “That's 13% of your Laptop goal”
• New challenges: No-spend weekends and the 52-week saving challenge (₹13,780 in a year). 5 new badges

Money 2.1.2 — tap your bank, don't type it 👆
• “Paid from” is now big buttons, two per row — each shows the bank, the account number and its balance (like Balance): 💵 Cash · 🏦 Union Bank ••1234 · 🏦 Federal ••5678 …
• Tap one and that account's balance goes down; delete the entry and it comes back
• Saying it works the natural way: “50 rupees for Lays, paid from Union Bank” or “cut from SBI”
• Replied to “Paid anyone?” by typing just “50 Ramesh”? The next notification has buttons with your other accounts — tap the right one, no bank names to type
• On any payment you added, the same buttons let you move it to another account

Money 2.1.1 — every rupee from the right account 🏦
• Adding a payment yourself now asks “Paid from”: 💵 Cash or the exact bank account — two Federal accounts show as Federal ••1234 and Federal ••5678, each with its balance
• That account's balance in Money changes right away (marked “+1 you added” until the bank confirms). Delete or hide the entry and the money goes back — cash wallet too
• Say it: “20 for chips from Federal ending 1234” or “10 for chips in cash” picks the account for you. If you only say “Federal” and you have two, it asks which one before saving
• Picked the wrong one? Open the payment → “Paid from ▾” → move it; both balances fix themselves
• Pay & note and the “Paid anyone?” reply also save to the account you choose (type “50 Ramesh from SBI”)
• A bank SMS that comes later joins your entry — never counted twice
