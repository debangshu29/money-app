Scan & Pay 📷
• New round Scan & Pay button in the middle of the bottom bar
• Scan any shop's UPI QR → Money already knows who it is (name + UPI ID)
• Pick the category, add a note → Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm opens with everything filled in
• When the bank's message arrives, the payment gets the name, category and note by itself
• Long-press the button to pay a UPI ID by typing it
