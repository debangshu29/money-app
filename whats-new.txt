Money 1.9.1 — fixes
• Scan & Pay: your UPI app now gets the shop's QR exactly as printed (no more “limit crossed” / “security reasons” from a rebuilt QR)
• Pick your UPI app once — Money remembers it
• If an app still refuses: “Scan in Google Pay” — scan the same QR there and Money still names the payment
• Amount is optional — type it in your UPI app if you like
• Small payments with no bank SMS (many banks skip SMS under ₹100): Money now reads payment-app notifications still showing when you open it, and understands more Google Pay / PhonePe wordings
• Settings → “Couldn't read” now also lists payment-app notifications it didn't understand — share them so the next version learns them
