Money 2.3 — Grow 🌱
• New Grow tab (Home · Activity · Insights · Plan · Grow). Pay & note is now the first button on Home
• Goals 2.0 in 3 taps: pick what for (emergency fund, phone, trip, festival…), how much and by when, then tap the bank account the money is in
• Money watches that account by itself: Ahead, On track or Behind by ₹X, how much you need a month, your real pace, and the month you'll reach it
• Graph of where each goal is heading — touch any month — plus What-if sliders: save ₹X more, or spend less on food delivery / shopping, and see how much sooner
• Split my saving: one slider for what you can save a month, shared fairly across your goals
• Last month's leftover: “₹4,200 was left in October — put it in a goal?”
• Festivals coming up (Durga Puja, Diwali, Christmas, Holi, Eid, Poila Baisakh) with what you spent last time — plan ahead in one tap
• Where goal money could sit (under 1 year, 1–3, 3–5, 5+ years) — for learning, not advice
• Goal photo, pause for a tough month, add / take out money, history
• Badges and celebrations at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% — even a notification the moment the bank SMS takes you over a milestone

Money 2.2 — clean & precise ✨
• New look: proper line icons everywhere (Home, Plan, Settings, insights) instead of emoji, and fewer words on every screen
• Home is simpler: “Needs you” (only when something does) · This month (spent, budget bar, safe today, today, came in) · Add / Say it / Accounts / Ask · Money you have · Recent
• Welcome tour: 8 short pages on what Money does — shown once now, and any time from Settings → Tutorials
• Settings → Tutorials: the tour plus short how-tos (how payments are caught, adding cash, accounts, budget, reminders, refunds, statements, privacy) with a “Show me” button
• Net worth and Rewards are gone; Plan is now Money · Life · Grow & tools (Grow gets much bigger in 2.3)

Money 2.1.2 — tap your bank, don't type it 👆
• “Paid from” is now big buttons, two per row — each shows the bank, the account number and its balance (like Balance): 💵 Cash · 🏦 Union Bank ••1234 · 🏦 Federal ••5678 …
• Tap one and that account's balance goes down; delete the entry and it comes back
• Saying it works the natural way: “50 rupees for Lays, paid from Union Bank” or “cut from SBI”
• Replied to “Paid anyone?” by typing just “50 Ramesh”? The next notification has buttons with your other accounts — tap the right one, no bank names to type
• On any payment you added, the same buttons let you move it to another account

Money 2.1.1 — every rupee from the right account 🏦
• Adding a payment yourself now asks “Paid from”: 💵 Cash or the exact bank account — two Federal accounts show as Federal ••1234 and Federal ••5678, each with its balance
• That account's balance in Money changes right away (marked “+1 you added” until the bank confirms). Delete or hide the entry and the money goes back — cash wallet too
• Say it: “20 for chips from Federal ending 1234” or “10 for chips in cash” picks the account for you. If you only say “Federal” and you have two, it asks which one before saving
• Picked the wrong one? Open the payment → “Paid from ▾” → move it; both balances fix themselves
• Pay & note and the “Paid anyone?” reply also save to the account you choose (type “50 Ramesh from SBI”)
• A bank SMS that comes later joins your entry — never counted twice
