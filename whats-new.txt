Never forget a payment 📍
• Saves where you paid (turn on in Settings → Remember every payment) — see it on a map from the payment
• Area names like “near Garia Station Road” (or map pin only, your choice)
• “Who was it?” — when a payment has no name, type it right in the notification
• Name a shop's QR once — every future payment to it gets the name and category
• “Probably Sharma Kirana?” — guesses from your past payments at the same spot / amount / time
• Add a bill photo to any payment
